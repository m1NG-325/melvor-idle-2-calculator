## 简体中文

- **修复中文名称显示**。使用原版游戏时，切到简体中文或繁體中文后，物品、行动、怪物和任务会优先显示游戏自带的中文名称，无需安装汉化补丁。
- **修复战斗配装计算**。双手武器的副手占位只算一次，已拥有的装备不会因此被误列进制作清单。也修正了回旋镖、投掷锤和提灯的数量与消耗估算。
- **配装推荐更清楚**。有变化的栏位会把「当前装备」和「推荐装备」上下列出。

v0.4.0 用户可在新版发布后点侧栏的「下载并更新」，原有设置会保留。

## English

- **Fixed Chinese game names.** On an unmodified game installation, switching to Simplified or Traditional Chinese now uses the game's built-in Chinese names for items, actions, enemies, and quests.
- **Fixed combat loadout calculations.** Two-handed weapons no longer count twice or make owned gear appear in the crafting list. Also corrected stack sizes and consumption estimates for boomerangs, throwing hammers, and lanterns.
- **Clarified gear recommendations.** Changed slots show current gear above recommended gear.

After release, v0.4.0 users can click **Download and Update** in the sidebar and keep their settings.
