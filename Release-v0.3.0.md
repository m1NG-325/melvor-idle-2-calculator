## 更新内容

- 经验收益分为「长时挂机」和「短时升级」。前者按挂机时段内的总 XP 排序，后者按达到目标等级的时间排序。库存不足时会显示还能获得多少 XP。战斗经验也区分整段平均和行动中速率。
- 新增任务材料页，自动展开当前任务步骤。交付材料按库存算缺口，指定行动按任务进度算剩余量。
- 材料产出按角色等级、可识别的加成和库存估算。任务和商店缺料时，都能比较不同途径的每小时产量和凑齐时间。
- 挂机收益分别显示整段平均和行动中的每小时净收益，方便看出缺料停机的影响。顶部推荐跟随筛选，保留名单里的掉落物不计入出售收入。离线收益按 24 小时上限结算。
- 战斗实测速率按怪物、区域和配装分别记录。没有匹配记录时，按当前区域和配装估算。

## What's new

- XP Gains now has Idle Session and Fastest Level-Up modes. Compare total XP over an idle session or find the fastest action to reach your target level. If stock runs out first, the tool shows how much XP you can still earn. Combat XP also shows both session-average and active rates.
- Added Quest Materials. The current quest step opens automatically. Hand-in shortages use your bank stock, and action goals use your saved progress.
- Material yields account for your levels, supported modifiers, and stock. Compare hourly yields and estimated time to get what you need from both quests and shop upgrades.
- Idle GP shows the full-session average and the rate while active, so you can see the effect of running out of materials. The top pick follows your filters, kept drops are excluded from sale income, and offline estimates use the 24-hour cap.
- Combat measurements are stored separately for each monster, area, and loadout. When there's no matching record, the tool estimates the rate for the selected area and current loadout.
