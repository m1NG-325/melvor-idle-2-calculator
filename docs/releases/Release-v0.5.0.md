## 简体中文

- **新增定时挂机。** 可以保存多个队列，拖动调整顺序，按时长、制作次数、物品数量、材料余量或技能等级结束。选「达到等级」时默认填入当前等级的下一级。
- **任务和产线可以加入队列。** 缺任务材料时可以刷够数量再切换。修复了中间步骤抢用后续配方所需材料，导致产线漏算的问题。
- **改进战斗收益和练级方案。** 副本里的祝祭或装备充能耗尽后，会按剩余加成继续估算。配装会比较已选附魔，并显示装备制作时间。等级有变化时直接显示 `38 → 41`。
- **移除启动投票。**

## English

- **Added Timed Action Queues.** Save multiple queues, drag to reorder steps, and set goals based on time, recipe completions, item counts, remaining materials, or skill levels. Level goals default to your next level.
- **Send quest materials and production chains to a queue.** Gather the required quantity before moving on. Production plans now reserve shared materials for later recipes.
- **Improved combat and training estimates.** Dungeon estimates adjust when prayers or equipment charges run out. Training plans compare your selected imbuement and show gear crafting time. Level gains appear as `38 → 41`.
- **Removed the startup poll.**
