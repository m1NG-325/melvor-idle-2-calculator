# v0.4.0 更新日志 / What’s New

## 简体中文

这版新增了「连锁产线」和「数据统计」，也补齐了几项技能的经验估算。选好角色存档，就能比较做什么更赚、练什么更快、缺的材料去哪拿。

- **连锁产线**：从采矿、冶炼到成品出售，逐步列出材料、耗时和产量。可以让工具推荐收益最高的方案，也可以自己选多个成品，在相同的时长和起始库存下对比。未解锁的步骤会标出来，使用库存和出售副产物也能单独设置。
- **经验收益**：补上灵巧、狩猎、农务和畜牧。技能经验和战斗经验分别查看，设定目标等级后即可估算还要练多久。技能配装会比较现有装备和灵巧商店装备，也会按剩余数量计算已支持的消耗型加成。
- **战斗练级**：选择想练的技能，工具会推荐怪物和配装，并比较现有装备与当前可制作的装备。如果制作装备不能让练级更快，就会提示无需制作。未解锁的目标会显示条件，无食物时会按剩余生命值估算能打多久。
- **任务与商店材料**：继续往下查配方所需的原料，以及这些原料的获取地点。等级不足、任务步骤未解锁等限制会标出来。修正了材料保留的算法，库存要够做一整批才能开工。
- **数据统计**：从存档查看游玩时长、行动次数、材料产出、战斗击杀和补给消耗等记录。可以按类别浏览，也可以搜索物品或指标。
- **界面与操作**：主题颜色和亮度会应用到各个页面，切换页面时背景渐变保持一致。挂机时长可填 1 分钟到 24 小时，上下箭头每次调整半小时。侧栏也可以参加本地工具和在线网站的偏好投票。

正在用 v0.3.0 的话，打开工具后点侧栏的「下载并更新」，原来的设置会保留。

## English

This update adds Production Chains and Statistics, along with estimates for more skills. Pick your save to compare what makes more GP, what gets you to your next level faster, and where to find missing materials.

- **Production Chains**: See the time, materials, and output at each step, from mining through smelting to selling the finished item. Get a recommendation or compare several items with the same time and starting stock. Locked steps are marked, and you can choose whether to use bank stock and sell leftover byproducts.
- **XP Gains**: Added Agility, Hunting, Farming, and Ranching. Skill XP and Combat XP have separate tabs, and you can set target levels to see how long they’ll take. Skill loadout recommendations compare gear you own with gear from the Agility shop. Supported consumable bonuses last only as long as your supply.
- **Combat Training**: Pick a skill to get a recommended enemy and loadout. Compare gear you own with gear you can craft. If crafting won’t make training faster, the tool says so. Locked encounters show their requirements, and food-free runs use your remaining HP to estimate how long you can fight.
- **Quest and Shop Materials**: Follow a recipe down to its ingredients and see where to get them. Level requirements and quest-step restrictions are marked. Ingredient preservation now applies only after you have enough stock to start a full batch.
- **Statistics**: Browse playtime, action counts, item production, kills, supply usage, and more from your save. Filter by category or search for an item or stat.
- **Interface and Controls**: Theme colors and brightness apply across pages, and the background gradient stays consistent when switching pages. Set idle times from 1 minute to 24 hours, with arrows that adjust the time by 30 minutes. The sidebar also has a poll on whether players prefer the local tool or a hosted website.

On v0.3.0, click **Download and Update** in the sidebar. Your settings carry over.
