## 简体中文

- 行动列队支持狩猎，显示当前步骤和目标进度，并估算每步耗时与每轮总时长。状态读取中断时会提示重试
- 商店升级可按资源或购买项目查看，勾选后可一键将缺少材料的产线加入行动列队
- 连锁产线按采集到成品的完整耗时计算每小时经验，并显示各技能的经验效率
- 经验收益可按游戏内分类筛选，任务分类排在最后。钓鱼行动标明主要产物，库存不足时同时显示可行动时间和到目标所需时间
- 数据统计新增行动次数、金币消耗、造成伤害、物品获得和消耗总计
- 补齐烹饪难度面板的漏译，最新版汉化按钮会变暗。Windows 启动时找不到 Node.js，可选择官网或国内镜像下载

## English

- Action Queue now supports Hunting, shows the current step and goal progress, and estimates time per step and per round. If status updates are interrupted, the app shows that it is retrying
- Shop upgrades can be grouped by resource or purchase. Select entries to add their missing-material production chains to the queue
- Production chains show XP per hour by skill, including time spent gathering ingredients
- Skill XP can be filtered by in-game category, with quest categories at the end. Fishing actions show their main catches, and material shortages show both available time and time needed
- Statistics now include totals for actions, gold spent, damage dealt, and items gained and consumed
- Filled translation gaps in the cooking difficulty panel and dimmed the patch button when it is up to date. The Windows launcher offers official and China mirror download links when Node.js is missing
